    About this atlas

    Ocean Life Atlas shows what people have recorded living in the sea. Click a spot to see the species found within a square about 150 km across, or search for an animal to see where it has been seen and how deep it lives.

    A list here is a record of what was found, not a census. Most of the ocean has hardly been sampled, so a short list usually means few people looked, not that little lives there. Switch on Where scientists have looked to see the gaps.

    Where the data comes from

    • Species records, ranges and depths: OBIS, the Ocean Biodiversity Information System (each dataset keeps its own licence, mostly CC BY or CC0).
    • Names and search: World Register of Marine Species (CC BY 4.0).
    • Photos: Wikimedia Commons, credited on each photo; common names and links: Wikidata (CC0), queried through QLever.
    • Summaries: Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0). Sea names: Marine Regions (CC BY 4.0).
    • Globe imagery: NASA Blue Marble through GIBS (public domain).
    • Red List status: IUCN categories as provided by OBIS.

    Everything loads live from these services in your browser; this site has no server and keeps no data about you.
